    Import items

    Add many items at once from an Excel, Google Sheets or CSV file.
    How to prepare your file
    1. 1Open Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers and put one item on each row.
    2. 2Type the column names in the first row, like the example below.
    3. 3Save it as .xlsx, .xls or .csv, then click Import.
    DescriptionQuantityRate
    Website design1500
    Monthly hosting1210

    The Amount is worked out for you (Quantity × Rate). If there's no quantity, it counts as 1.

    Other column names that also work
    • Description: Item, Name, Product
    • Quantity: Qty, Hours
    • Rate: Price, Unit price
    • Amount: Total, Line total
    • SKU: Code, Item code, Product code

    Capital letters, column order and extra columns don't matter.

    Help & Support

    Getting started

    1. Add your business detailsClick any field on the invoice, or use Form → Business profile. Upload your logo there too.
    2. Fill in the client and line itemsTotals, tax and discount update as you type.
    3. Choose a designPick a template, colors and page size in the Design panel.
    4. Save or printEverything autosaves on this device. Use Save to keep a copy under Saved Invoices, and Print to print or save as PDF.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Redo (or Ctrl + Y)

    On a Mac, use ⌘ Cmd instead of Ctrl.

    Good to know

    • Edit, Form or PreviewEdit lets you type straight onto the page. Form shows simple fields. Preview shows exactly what will print.
    • Table columnsUse Edit columns (or the ⋮ on a column) to rename, hide, reorder or resize columns. Drag the cards to change the order.
    • Import itemsAdd many items at once from Excel, Google Sheets or a CSV file. Open “How to prepare your file” for an example and a sample file.
    • Brand templatesSave your company details and design once, then reuse them for every new invoice or another business.
    • CurrencyClick the currency box to choose from the list, click it again to type and search.
    • Install as an appInstall it from your browser's menu or the install banner. It then works fully offline.

    Your data

    Invoices, templates and settings are stored only in this browser on this device — nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes them, so print or save important invoices as PDF.
